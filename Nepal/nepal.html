Welcome to Explore Nepal

Nepal is a beautiful country located in South Asia. It is famous for Mount Everest, rich culture, ancient temples, and friendly people. Thousands of tourists visit Nepal every year to enjoy its natural beauty and historical places.

National Flag

Famous Places

1. Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. It attracts climbers from all over the globe.

2. Pokhara

Pokhara is known for its beautiful lakes, mountain views, and adventure activities like paragliding and boating.

3. Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and is a major pilgrimage site for Buddhists around the world.

4. Chitwan National Park

Chitwan National Park is famous for one-horned rhinoceroses, elephants, tigers, and jungle safaris.

5. Kathmandu Durbar Square

Kathmandu Durbar Square is a UNESCO World Heritage Site filled with old palaces, courtyards, and temples built by the Malla kings.

6. Bhaktapur Durbar Square

Bhaktapur is an ancient city known for its well-preserved medieval art, wood carvings, and pottery square.

7. Annapurna Region

The Annapurna region offers some of the most popular trekking routes in the world, with stunning views of the Himalayas.

8. Janakpur

Janakpur is a sacred city believed to be the birthplace of Goddess Sita, home to the beautiful Janaki Temple.

Travel Tips

Best Time to Visit

Any month of the year offers clear skies and pleasant weather, making it the best time for trekking and sightseeing.

Visa Information

Most tourists can get a visa on arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport or land border crossings by paying a fee and providing a passport photo.

Currency

The official currency is the Nepalese Rupee (NPR). It is helpful to carry some cash since not all places accept cards.

Language

Nepali is the official language, though many people in tourist areas also speak English.

What to Pack

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Warm clothing for mountain areas
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Basic first aid kit

Transportation

Tourists can travel by domestic flights, tourist buses, taxis, or hired vehicles to reach different cities and trekking areas.

Safety Tips

  • Drink only bottled or purified water
  • Carry travel insurance for trekking trips
  • Respect local customs and dress modestly at temples
  • Acclimatize slowly when trekking at high altitudes
